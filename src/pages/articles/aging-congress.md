A final project for a Data Visualization course, created between 3 people. My focuses were on data collection + cleaning, the style of the main visualization of all members of congress + senate, and the interactive timeline. The project was my first introduction to Svelte and svg paths. I could go either way on Svelte but this made me realize how useful svgs are from a design perspective. This website would not have been possible to make in the style that I would have wanted without copious svgs.

I really enjoyed working on this project as it gave me the chance to get better at front end while working on a problem I think is really important to pay attention as a citizen of the United States of America. It was quite motivating (and gratifying after the fact) to make what I believe to be an important visualization and to make it intractable. 

This is currently hosted on a CloudFlare domain that I maintain, so you can [check it out](https://aging-congress.us/) now!
