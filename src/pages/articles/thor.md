I am a [reformed gamer]("/aricles/thor"), well I'm at least off the hard stuff. I have never really been into the competitive scene, mostly because I am genuinely bad at FPS games - to such an embarrassing degree that I avoid them entirely. I am much more interested in story telling, cinematics / graphical aesthetic, and good music is always a plus. 

All games I play came out 10-20 years ago. I admit that much of my preference is out of nostalgia, but some of them I am experiencing for the first time now. I really love this era of games. They feel as though the team creating them had genuine passion, which in tandem with the technical limitations and the ways these teams chose to tackle them, gave each game a unique aesthetic.

This machine also has a terabyte of storage, so beyond holding every single Wii game that I have ever owned, it also holds DVD rips of some of my favorite shows and movies, keeping with the theme of nostalgia. For instance I bought the Adventure Time CD Boxset and ripped every disks just so I could have it on this device. I also added a rip of 1 of my 6 copies of Happy Feet.

This was the first machine I have had in a very long time to be an android device. I switched to apple for the soul reason of being allowed on the family group chat a number of years ago. I used to always be a galaxy guy until my galaxy s 7 got a crack on the side of the curved screen. Neadless to say that was the last time I would have a non otterbox like construction worker level protective case on any of my phones.
