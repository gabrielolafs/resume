I gave this to myself as a graduation gift after high school. I was obsessed with the [moonlander](https://www.zsa.io/moonlander) for some time before this. I wanted to buy my end game keyboard as my first expensive keyboard, and with that I have succeeded.

I chose an ortholinear keyboard with the [Dvorak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dvorak_keyboard_layout) keyboard layout because I knew my future would consist of mostly deskwork, and with a computer science degree, I knew I would be spending much of the rest of my life typing away. Dvorak is specifically made so that you don't have to move your fingers from the home row as often, resulting in less potential for carpal tunnel syndrome. The ortholinear arrangement is also supposed to reduce the risk for carpal tunnel. I've heard war stories about dealing with carpal tunnel syndrome from the person who gave me and my sister a free switch as a result, so not all bad for us.

That summer between high school and college (a summer of unemployment I look back on fondly, for almost two weeks I was on a 36/12 sleep schedule, I had very important Minecraft skyblock worlds to maintain) I taught myself the Dvorak keyboard layout. This was an overcorrection for never truly learning how to touch type. 

This new keyboard layout came with a steep learning curve. If I had a job that summer, or really anything better to do, I don't know if the time commitment would be worth it (for evidence here is my [MonkeyType Profile](https://monkeytype.com/profile/gabeo), at the beginning I was using SZA's own website [typ.ing](https://typ.ing) which is an incredibly well thought out and well made website. It was such an ideal way to learn this new keyboard, and this software solidified my love for SZA). I believe in jumping in the deep end to learn to swim, so the first few days I was typing much much less, starting at a snail’s pace, and that was after an hour or two of figuring out where the keys even were. 

To this day, I still look down every once in a while to regather my bearings on a qwerty keyboard, but surprisingly, my brain can easily flip-flop between the layouts.
