My apartment in Worcester was not a place for beauty. It was a place to sleep at night, and in the winter months, under 4 blankets. For the sake of my roommates, the networking had to be easily deployed and functional, but I also wanted the ability to mess around a little. As is a common theme with me, it had to be a good deal or I would be tempted to make things myself. After a conversation with a coworker who's job it is to be a good network engineer, I opted for a Ubiquity setup, and I could not be more happy with the decision. Instead of renting a router from our ISP for two years, I chose to spend 20 dollars more up front for an Express 7 router, a Lite 8 PoE switch, and 2 U6+ Access Points. Plus I kept it all after the fact, deploying them in my (home home lab)[/articles/home-network]

##### Pi-Hole:
This is my local solution for DNS. I use it to access the things like the vnc connection to [[#Prorm (pro~~g~~r~~am~~m~~er~~ PC)|Prorm]], that one is at prorm.com. My biggest sell for a [Pi Hole](https://pi-hole.net/) is that it can be configured as a network wide ad-blocker, essentially filtering out known advertising DNS resolutions.

##### CopyParty PC:
I needed something to essentially function as a NAS but on a laptop that I acquired for extremely cheap right after the Windows 10 EOL and right before the great RAM shortage. I was thinking of learning TrueNAS for this but it has 500g of storage (less than the [[#Parent Pod - 512g|Parent Pod]]...) and only on one ssd, so it really felt like overkill and I could not even use any of the redundancy features. This is mostly for transferring files so redundancy is not all to important anyways. I use to use [CopyParty](https://github.com/9001/copyparty), which is a single python file that does everything for me. The coolest thing about CopyParty is its ability to start downloading before the file has fully been transferred, which is very useful for if I am uploading from wifi and downloading from something hard lined, as it is downloaded seconds after it is fully done uploading.
